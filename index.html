每日清相簿 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 6 日

「每日清相簿」（以下稱「本 app」）每天從你的相簿隨機挑出幾張相片，讓你決定保留或刪除。本 app 不蒐集、不上傳、也不分享你的任何個人資料。

相片

  • 本 app 需要你允許取用相片，才能顯示相片、在你確認後刪除，以及在你點愛心時把相片加到喜愛項目或從中移除。
  • 相片只會在你的裝置上讀取與顯示，不會傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。
  • 只有在你按下確認，並在系統跳出的視窗再次同意後，相片才會被刪除。在 iOS 上，刪除的相片會先移到「最近刪除」。
  • 只有在你主動長按相片並選擇分享時，相片才會交給系統的分享功能，傳送到你自己選擇的對象或 app。

儲存在裝置上的資料

以下資料只儲存在你的裝置上，移除本 app 時會一併刪除：

  • 你的設定（每天張數、提醒時間）。
  • 使用紀錄（完成的天數、連續天數、成就、整理過的張數）。
  • 當天挑出的相片的識別碼與你做的決定，讓你可以中途離開再回來繼續。
  • 你選擇保留的相片的識別碼，用來避免短期內重複出現。

通知

如果你開啟每日提醒，本 app 會在你的裝置上排定本機通知。通知不經過任何伺服器，本 app 也不使用遠端推播。

我們不做的事

  • 不需要註冊帳號。
  • 不使用廣告、分析或追蹤工具。
  • 不將任何資料提供給第三方。

兒童隱私

本 app 不蒐集任何使用者的個人資料，包含兒童。

政策變更

如果本政策有所更新，會公布在這個頁面，並更新上方的日期。

Daily Photo Sweep (每日清相簿) Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Daily Photo Sweep ("the app") shows you a few random photos from your library each day so you can decide to keep or delete them. The app does not collect, upload or share any personal data.

Photos

  • The app needs access to your photo library in order to show your photos, to delete the ones you choose, and to add a photo to Favorites or remove it when you tap the heart.
  • Photos are read and displayed on your device only. They are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Photos are deleted only after you confirm in the app and again in the system prompt. On iOS, deleted photos first move to "Recently Deleted".
  • A photo is handed to the system share sheet only when you press and hold it and choose to share it, and it goes only to the recipient or app you pick.

Data stored on your device

The following is stored on your device only and is removed when you delete the app:

  • Your settings (photos per day, reminder time).
  • Usage records (days completed, streak, achievements, number of photos reviewed).
  • Identifiers of the photos picked for the day and your decisions, so you can leave and continue later.
  • Identifiers of photos you chose to keep, so they are not shown again too soon.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No server is involved and the app does not use remote push notifications.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any update to this policy will be published on this page with a new date above.
